# Marketing Analytics & Reporting Portfolio

## Overview

This project demonstrates how data analytics and reporting tools can be used to monitor marketing performance, evaluate business KPIs, and support data-driven decision-making.

The portfolio includes two complementary projects: a **social media performance dashboard for Mamaearth** built in Power BI and an **internal marketing agency reporting dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel. Together, they cover external campaign performance as well as internal departmental performance monitoring.

## Projects Included

### 1. Mamaearth Social Media Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

**Objective:** Analyze social media performance across Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube to understand audience reach, engagement, content performance, and campaign effectiveness.

**Key areas of analysis:**
- Social media performance across three platforms.
- Reach, views, engagement, and other platform-specific metrics.
- Monthly performance trends and month-over-month comparisons.
- Content and platform performance analysis.
- KPI monitoring to identify opportunities for improving marketing strategy.

**Business value:** Helps evaluate which platforms and content strategies perform better, identify performance trends, and support more informed social media planning.

**Tools:** Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, DAX

### 2. Internal Marketing Agency Performance Report — Microsoft Excel

**Objective:** Develop an internal reporting solution to monitor departmental KPIs, compare actual performance against targets, and improve visibility into agency operations.

**Key areas of analysis:**
- Department-wise performance monitoring.
- Actual-versus-target KPI comparisons.
- Marketing channel and business performance tracking.
- Identification of performance gaps and areas requiring attention.
- Consolidated reporting to support management review and operational decisions.

**Departments covered:** Creative, Sales, Analytics, HR, and IT.

**Business value:** Helps management review departmental performance, identify gaps against targets, and make reporting and resource-planning decisions using a consolidated view of key metrics.

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, formulas, KPI reporting, dashboard visualization

## Skills Demonstrated
